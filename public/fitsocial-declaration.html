Preamble

Why I wrote this down

Faith that only makes sense after the fact isn't faith. So I am writing the whole thing down now — while the numbers are small, while the office is wherever I open my laptop, while most people still have to ask what FitSocial is.

Every line below is written in the present tense on purpose. Not "I hope." Not "one day." This is what is happening. My job is to move like it's already true, work like it depends on me, and pray like it depends on God — because it does.

I am building a company that makes the world healthier. Physically, mentally, socially. That is the assignment. The buildings, the money, the recognition — those are the receipts, not the reason.

I will read this until it stops sounding crazy to me. Then I will read it until it stops sounding crazy to everyone else.

I

The Mission

What FitSocial is on this earth to do

  1. FitSocial is the #1 app in the App Store for Health & Fitness — not for a day of hype, but held, defended, and earned by being the one people actually keep on their home screen. Number one because it works. The ranking is a byproduct of the transformation.
  2. Millions of lives are measurably transformed — weight lost, sickness reversed, confidence rebuilt, depression lifted, families changed because one person in the house got healthy first.
  3. Millions of monthly paying subscribers worldwide — people who gladly pay because FitSocial gave them back their body, their energy, and their years.
  4. FitSocial makes fitness social again — no one does the hard thing alone. The app turns strangers into training partners and training partners into lifelong friends.
  5. We are profitable, abundant, and growing year over year — a business so healthy it never has to compromise the mission to survive.
  6. We do good in the world, on purpose — free access where it's needed, health brought to communities that were written off, and a company people are proud to be associated with.

III

The Grounds

The FitSocial Club · a country club for everybody

We build the greatest fitness campuses in the world — and we open the gates. A country club feeling without the country club gate: elite trainers, real doctors, real food, real community, in multiple cities worldwide.

  1. Multiple sports, real fields — basketball, football, soccer, track, tennis, pickleball, swimming. Places where grown men and women get to play again.
  2. Elite coaches and trainers — the caliber pros fly across the country for, available to the person who just decided to start.
  3. On-site doctors, nurses, and recovery — bloodwork, physical therapy, sauna, cold plunge, sleep and stress care. Health, not just exercise.
  4. A food hall that makes eating well the easy choice — food so good people come for the meal and stay for the workout.
  5. Open to everybody — the kid from the neighborhood and the CEO train in the same building. That is the whole point.
If the vision doesn't scare me, it's too small to need God.
The rule I build by
IV

The People

The company everybody wants to work for

  1. Our people are paid extraordinarily well — salary, equity, health, time, and dignity. Nobody who builds this company has to worry about money at home.
  2. FitSocial is a dream job — the résumé line people are proud of forever, the place the best in the world quietly hope to get a call from.
  3. Our team is healthy — we would be frauds otherwise. The people building the health company live the health company.
  4. Major sports leagues, brands, and institutions partner with us — because we became the standard, not because we begged.
  5. Leaders are grown here — people leave FitSocial (rarely) as better builders, better spouses, better humans than they arrived.
V

The Man I Become

Character first · everything else follows

  1. I am named to the Forbes 30 Under 30 — and the recognition changes nothing about how early I get up.
  2. I become a billionaire — a Black billionaire who opens doors on the way up and refuses to close them behind him.
  3. I am a wealthy, healthy, prosperous man — strong body, clear mind, settled spirit. I am the walking advertisement for what I sell.
  4. I am an extraordinary speaker — when I speak, rooms lean in. My energy changes the temperature of a place before I say a word.
  5. I am the kind of man other men study — how I lead, how I love my wife, how I keep my word. Men want to build what I've built and become what I've become.
  6. My integrity is not for sale — I am the same man in the boardroom, in my house, and when nobody is watching.
  7. My circle is elite and real — founders, CEOs, athletes, artists, and people operating at the top of their craft. Iron sharpens iron; I bring as much as I take.
  8. I stay teachable — I keep mentors, keep reading, keep asking better questions. Arrogance is the fastest way to lose everything on this page.
VI

The House of Oyadiran

The thing I refuse to sacrifice for the company

Lajoie Divine King becomes Lajoie Divine Oyadiran. We build a marriage that is happy, honest, deeply loving, and unshakable — a partnership people point to and say that's what I want.

We raise beautiful, healthy, brilliant children who know God, know their name, and know they were wanted. They grow up watching a father who worked hard and still came home.

My wife never has to wonder if she chose right. My children never have to compete with my calendar for my attention. Whatever I build out there, they get the best of me — not the leftovers.

I change the trajectory of the Oyadiran name. What was hard for the generations before me becomes normal for the generations after me. My grandchildren inherit not just money but standards, faith, and a working machine.

VII

The Legacy

What is still standing when I'm gone

  1. FitSocial outlives me, my children, and my grandchildren — built on systems and values, not on my personality.
  2. The world is measurably healthier because this company existed — that sentence is the only success metric that ultimately counts.
  3. My family's name means something for generations — trust, excellence, generosity, faith.
  4. The wealth is a tool — it funds churches, schools, gyms, scholarships, and families who never hear my name.
  5. God gets the credit — I never rewrite this story with myself as the source of it.
